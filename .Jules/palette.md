## 2025-02-19 - The Importance of Feedback
**Learning:** Even without a backend, users expect immediate feedback on form interaction. A static form that just reloads feels broken.
**Action:** Always implement simulated feedback (loading/success states) for static prototypes to maintain trust.

## 2025-02-19 - Invisible Labels
**Learning:** "Minimalist" designs often omit labels for aesthetics, hurting accessibility.
**Action:** If visual redesign isn't possible, `aria-label` matching the placeholder is a critical non-negotiable fallback.
