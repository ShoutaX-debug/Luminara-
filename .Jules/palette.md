## 2025-05-18 - Accessibility in Placeholder-First Designs
**Learning:** This project relies heavily on placeholders instead of visible labels for forms to maintain a sleek, minimal aesthetic. While this looks good, it's a major accessibility barrier.
**Action:** When visual redesign isn't an option, explicitly adding `aria-label` attributes that match the placeholder text is a critical "invisible" fix that brings the site into compliance without breaking the visual style.
