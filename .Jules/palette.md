# Palette's Journal

## 2025-10-26 - Contact Form & Footer Accessibility
**Learning:** Forms relying solely on `placeholder` attributes are invisible to screen readers without `aria-label`, even if visually clear to sighted users.
**Action:** Always verify inputs have accessible labels (either `<label>` or `aria-label`) during the "Observe" phase, especially in design-heavy or "minimalist" layouts.
